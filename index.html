SUPERNOVA 2026 // TERMS & CONDITIONS POLICY
Official Event Regulations, Telemetry Guidelines & Academic Conduct
groups Team Composition & Eligibility

SUPERNOVA 2026 strictly admits Duo (2 Hackers) or Trio (3 Hackers) teams. Solo participation is prohibited to preserve collaborative problem-solving dynamics. Every member must be a currently enrolled college student carrying a valid institutional ID card for physical check-in.

lan Capacity Quotas & Slot Limits

To ensure deep technical mentorship, hardware allocation, and dedicated jury evaluation, strict track quotas are enforced:

  • Web Debugging (Arena 01): Strictly capped at 15 team slots.
  • AI / ML Intelligence (Arena 02): Strictly capped at 25 team slots.

Once quotas are claimed in the database, registration automatically closes for that track. Single-track registration is strictly enforced.

laptop_mac Hardware Policy (BYOD)

Participants must bring their own laptops (BYOD - Bring Your Own Device), chargers, and necessary adapters. High-speed campus Wi-Fi and power strips are provided at every workstation.

payments Registration Fee Structure

A flat event fee of ₹300 per team applies uniformly across both Duo (2) and Trio (3) rosters for either competition arena. No additional fees are charged during the event.

qr_code_scanner Payment Proof & Verification Protocol

After Step 1 registration, teams transfer ₹300 via the official organizer UPI QR code (swainpapa@fam) and upload their transfer screenshot with UTR reference. Pass clearance and QR credentials unlock once admin review approves the transaction.

cancel Non-Refundable Policy

All registrations and entry fees are strictly non-refundable once allocated. Because slots are hard-capped (15 Web, 25 AI/ML), reserving a slot permanently locks out other applicants and dedicates logistical resources to your team.

workspace_premium ₹6,000 Total Prize Pool Allocation

The confirmed prize purse of ₹6,000 will be awarded to top podium finishers based on cumulative jury scores:

  • AI / ML System: Cash awards up to ₹4,000 for 1st Place.
  • Web Debugging: Cash purse up to ₹2,000 for top bug resolution speed and accuracy.
military_tech Top 10 Internship Interview Pathways

The Top 10 verified student rankers across both arenas receive guaranteed technical interview opportunities with partner engineering firms.

Legal Disclaimer: The internship pathway guarantees direct interview access and prioritized evaluation. Final internship offers are contingent upon the candidate clearing partner technical rounds, background checks, and meeting individual corporate hiring policies.

verified Academic Integrity & Original Code

All work debugged, modeled, or written during the hackathon must be the original contribution of team members. Plagiarism, copying pre-written solutions, or sharing answers between competing teams results in immediate disqualification.

security Cybersecurity & Network Regulations

Attacking competition servers, probing other teams' machines, intercepting wireless traffic, or attempting DDoS on the telemetry cluster is strictly prohibited and grounds for immediate eviction and academic reporting.

handshake Campus Decorum

Participants must respect college staff, student coordinators, and venue property at Tolani College of Commerce. The decisions of the jury and organizing committee are final and binding.
